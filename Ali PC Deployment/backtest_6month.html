How to read this

Drawdown is measured on executed equity — realised cash plus open-position float at every M15 bar — not on closed trades only, so it reflects what the account balance actually did intra-trade.

Costs are modelled, not measured. $0.52 spread and $0.20 stop slippage per trade are realistic for XAUUSDm, but a fast market widens both. At 4,451 trades, every extra $0.10 of cost removes $445.

Regime dependency. This window's median M15 range is $8.48, which is unusually wide for gold. A stacking trend-follower thrives in that; the ATR-scaled stop adapts the rule if volatility falls, but these dollar returns should not be expected in a calmer market.

Entry-candle assumption. Where a trade opens and closes inside one candle, M15 OHLC cannot say whether the high or the low came first. This test assumes the adverse extreme comes first, which is the pessimistic reading.
